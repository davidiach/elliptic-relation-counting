\paragraph{Status.}
This note proves a composition conditional on the supplied finite-core,
uniform packing, mode-entropy, arithmetic-encoding, and ECDLP-reduction
theorems. It does not independently establish those inherited theorems.
The new programs check finite interfaces and rational inequalities;
they are not a formal verification of the asymptotic conclusion.
The common comparison class is nonsingular short Weierstrass curves over
prime fields $p>3$, with supplied order $hr$, odd prime $r$, coprime
polynomially bounded $h$, a supplied order-$r$ point, and targets in its
subgroup. Put $b=\lceil\log_2p\rceil$.

\section{Named premises and the new composition}
The following supplied components are used as premises:
\begin{enumerate}
\item The C38 alternating-anchor compiler in the profiled archive,
including parsimony, canonical Fourier words, symmetric abscissa hashes,
the original target law, degree bounds, and complete relation extraction.
\item The orientation-sensitive packet allocation and adaptive
nine-group pair-sector theorem in the sharper archive, including
deterministic packing, entropy estimates, rational matrix lifting,
and recursive resource bounds.
\item The integral simplex selector and uniform compiler argument in
the simplex archive. The uniform extension to the sharper schedule
and its pair sectors is given below, rather than inferred from a
fixed-alphabet theorem.
\item The capped relation-collection and verified sparse-extraction
reduction, with enumeration budget $B=2^{b/m+O(\log b)}$ and cost
$\operatorname{poly}(b,m)(BC_m+mB^2)$.
\end{enumerate}
The new composition replaces the C44 signed encoding in the simplex
application by C38 and replaces its contraction constant by the
adaptive-pair constant. It retains the C38 point-label convention;
no signed-column rank assumption is imported from the C44 branch.

\section{Recoding the actual C38 word boundaries}
Write $v=\lfloor(m-1)/2\rfloor$. The inherited compiler has
$r_f=5m-5+2v$ forward arrays and $r_i=3m-2+v$ inverse arrays.
There are exactly
\[
 (r_f+r_i)L=(8m-7+3v)L
\]
Fourier words. At index $(j_2,t_1)$ a forward word joins $F_{j_2}$
to $M_{t_1}$, and an inverse word joins $M_{t_1}$ to
$E_{\lfloor j_2/R\rfloor}$. Every digit of a word has the same
two endpoints. Let $M_F$ be the Fourier modulus and
$W=\lceil\log_2 M_F\rceil$; the inherited construction gives
$LW=(4+o(1))b$ and $W=\Theta(\log^2b)$.

Choose once at the root
\[
 t=\max(2,\lfloor\log_2\log_2(b+4)\rfloor),\quad
 \mathcal A_t=\{x\in\mathbb Z_{\ge0}^t:|x|\le t\},\quad
 Q=\binom{2t}{t}.
\]
Rank the joint symbols by stars and bars. Replace each Fourier word by
$\lceil\log_Q M_F\rceil$ symbols. Each incident predicate decodes
its own incident words, rejects values at least $M_F$, and performs
the original integer arithmetic. Binary side states use two fixed
symbols; all other letters are rejected at these ports.

\begin{lemma}
This recoding preserves accepted assignments bijectively, including
recovered factor tuples and relation existence. If $S$ is the number
of recoded edges, then
\[
 tS\le(19+o(1))mb.
\]
Maximum degree and parallel multiplicity remain within the inherited
low-degree root class, up to polylogarithmic factors.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Canonical integer representations at both endpoints give inverse
encoding and decoding maps. They require no conversion tensor or
additional witness. The exact number of word edges is
$(8m-7+3v)L\lceil\log_QM_F\rceil$.

In the general-prime compiler the side-edge count is at most
\[
 S_{\rm aux}\le
 2r_i\left\lfloor\frac{L-1}{R}\right\rfloor W_c
 +2(r_f+m-1)(n-1),
\]
where $W_c$ is the signed carry width, $W_c=O(\log^2b)$,
$n=O(L)$, and $R=\Theta(\log b)$. Hash carry chains only shorten
the first term. Consequently $S_{\rm aux}=O(mb/\log b)$.
This is a quantitative estimate: $tS_{\rm aux}=o(mb)$ even
after the nonconstant alphabet cost is charged. Word rounding costs
$O(mL)$ edges, and $tmL=o(mb)$.
Finally,
\[
 \frac{t}{\log_2\binom{2t}{t}}
 =\frac12+O\left(\frac{\log t}{t}\right).
\]
The leading Boolean word ledger is $(38+o(1))mb$, proving the
claim. All recoded endpoints are unchanged; the new descriptions,
degree conversion from $b$ to $S$, and multiplicities incur only
polylogarithmic factors. Counts and witnesses therefore induce the
same C38 relation distribution when the finder uses the same
deterministic selection rule, or a correspondingly coupled rule.
The coverage argument needs existence and is unaffected by recoding.
\end{proof}

\section{A uniform simplex lift of the sharper contractor}
For $y\in\mathcal A_t$, append $y_0=t-|y|$ and define
\[
 L_y(z)=\frac{t!}{\prod_{j=0}^t y_j!}
 (t-\textstyle\sum_{j=1}^tz_j)_{y_0}
 \prod_{j=1}^t(z_j)_{y_j}.
\]
Here $(a)_k$ is a falling factorial. These integral polynomials
have degree $t$ and satisfy $L_y(x)=t!\,1_{x=y}$ on the joint
alphabet. Coordinates are not independent graph edges.

The existing deformation is applied in each of $t$ coordinates.
This produces $t$ copies of each crossing family and multiplies
the leading Hermite mass by $t$. Every unordered group pair still
has exactly $1/36$ of the padded families: all coordinates have
identical group incidences. Pair-sector entropies and projected-child
entropies thus acquire a factor $\sqrt{t\,t}=t$.

\begin{proposition}[Uniform lift under the inherited core premises]
For root-fixed $t=O(\log\log S)$, the sharper contractor extends to
the joint simplex alphabet with
\[
 \log_2 T(S)\le(tK+o(t))\frac S{\sqrt{\log_2S}},\qquad
 \log_2\operatorname{Space}(S)\le(tJ+o(t))\frac S{\sqrt{\log_2S}}.
\]
Here $K,J$ are its Boolean adaptive-pair coefficients, or the
modified matrix coefficients certified in the next section.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Use the sharper schedule with $\lambda=\lceil\log_2S\rceil$,
$\Gamma=\lceil12\log_2\lambda\rceil$, and the largest $u$ satisfying
\[
 2^{a_{\rm prev}+\Gamma}90^u\lambda^{48}\le S,
 \qquad a=a_{\rm prev}+u+\Gamma.
\]
Its orientation-sensitive allocation depends only on graph endpoints
and labels, so it is unchanged by coordinate replication. Its strict
degree and multiplicity margins, active depth $O(\log\log S)$,
and packet gap $\lambda-a=\Omega(\lambda^\eta)$ for fixed $\eta>0$
remain available. Direct contraction handles failed finite guards.

At a call with $M$ edges set
$q_i=\lceil tM(u-i)/(u-2)\rceil$ for $0\le i<u$, zero otherwise.
The exact Hermite count exceeds degree $tM(2L-1)$ for $u\ge4$.
Let $\nu=v_2(t!)$. The sufficient working precision is
\[
 P_i=p+\nu M+\sum_{j=1}^{a-i}j2^{a-i-j}(q_i-q_{i+j})
           +q_i(a+1-i)+1.
\]
The extra $\nu M$ is charged at every recursive call. Reconstruct
$(t!)^M$ times the contraction, divide its canonical coefficients
by $2^{\nu M}$, and multiply by the inverse of the odd remaining
factor. No nonunit is inverted.

The inherited precision induction extends to
$p\le C tS\lambda^2 2^{a_{\rm prev}}$ for an absolute sufficiently
large $C$: $q_i\le2tM$, the loss is at most $4tMD_i$, and
$D_i\ge2^{a_{\rm prev}+\Gamma+1}$. Relative to $tMD_i/u$,
the inherited precision, scalar loss, Taylor correction, and rounding
are uniformly negligible. The leading precision remains
$(6t+o(t))MD_i/u$.

For pair $e$ with exact weight $p_e$, its entropy bound is now
$(tH/6)\sqrt{p_e/P}+o(tH)$, with the original Boolean $H$.
The partition-estimate error from $t$ replicated mode families is
$O(t(M/D)\log^2S)=o(tM/\sqrt u)$ uniformly. There are only 36
pair bundles. Fixing exact degree per pair as in the executable
adapter adds at most $(tM+1)^{36}$ sectors; weight sectors add
$(P+1)^{36}$. Across $O(\log\log S)$ levels their logarithmic
overhead is $O(\log S\log\log S)$.

The depth-first packet coefficient cost has logarithm
$O(tL\log(tL))$. The native coefficient dimension is at most
$(2tS)^{O(\log\log S)}$. Both are lower order by the quantitative
packet gap. Direct leaves have $M\le S/\lambda$ and cost
$Q^M=2^{O(tS/\lambda)}$. Original tensor evaluation remains
polynomial. Root counting precision is $O(tS)$ since $\log_2Q\le2t$.
Formula descriptions, denominator guards, and bit operations retain
their charged polynomial or quasipolynomial bounds. In particular,
the finite fixed-alphabet theorem is not being applied with an
unstated alphabet-dependent constant.

Every local join and child continuation is multiplied by $t$.
Thus the inherited strict recurrence margin is preserved. Sequential
processing of sectors and descendants gives the stated storage bound;
the optional cache is not necessary for this space argument.
\end{proof}

\section{Improving the matrix parameters with exact checks}
The published-source row uses the verified numerical consequences
\[
 \alpha=0.32133<0.321334,\qquad
 \Omega=2.37134>2.371339.
\]
The dual exponent comes from \cite{wxxz}; the stronger square bound
comes from \cite{alman}. Both inequalities have slack. The inherited
verified-menu construction supplies rational formulas with vanishing
accuracy loss and charges their search. Put
\[
 \beta=\frac{\Omega-2}{1-\alpha}=\frac{37134}{67867},\qquad
 d=1-\alpha\beta.
\]
For sorted logarithmic dimensions $x\le y\le z$, the limiting
rectangular bound is $\max(y+z,z+dy+\beta x)$.
The inherited balanced-partition averaging matrix has diagonal,
adjacent, and disjoint counts $210,60,40$ out of 280, and row
sum $27/4$. Hence a selected triple has boundary at most
$B=\sqrt{27/2}$ for unit pair-weight norm.

For an internal join, the support problem has
$x^2/2+y^2/12+z^2/6\le1$ and $y+z\le B$.
For a coefficient permutation $(a,b,c)$ set
$m=(2b+c)/3$ and $R^2=2a^2+4(b-c)^2$. Its upper bound is
\[
 \Psi(m,R^2)=
 \begin{cases}
 m\sqrt{27/2}+R/2,&m^2\ge R^2/6,\\
 \sqrt{18m^2+R^2},&m^2<R^2/6.
 \end{cases}
\]
This follows by maximizing $mS+R\sqrt{1-S^2/18}$ over $0\le S\le B$.
The second case must be retained when changing matrix inputs.

Four of the six coefficient permutations are below $\sqrt{14}$.
The two remaining forms for each choice of isolated vertex are
averaged over all eight selections of one form per vertex, as in
the inherited proof. The new certificate derives their six-coordinate
vectors directly and checks their support bounds with rational
intervals. This gives
\[
 C_*=(2+d)\sqrt{3/2}+\beta/\sqrt3
      =3.7748039717\ldots.
\]
The first merge is bounded by $\sqrt{14}$ using
$\beta^2+7d^2+7<14$. The top join is bounded by $\sqrt{27/2}$
using $\beta^2+d^2<1$. Thus the entire tree is controlled.
Writing
\[
 B_9=\frac{2\pi}{\ln2}\sqrt{\tfrac49\log_2 90},
 \quad K=\frac{C_*B_9}{6},\quad J=\frac{\sqrt{14}B_9}{6},
\]
the certificates give $K=9.6870299622\ldots$, $J=9.6019680719\ldots$,
and projected continuation $9.5707909081\ldots<J<K$.

The finite implementation uses rational square-root approximations
for tree selection. If each $\sqrt{p_e}$ is rounded down with error
at most $\varepsilon$, each nine-edge bundle sum loses at most
$9\varepsilon$. When $\sum_ep_e\le P$, choosing the approximate
minimizer increases the exact partition objective by at most
$162\sqrt P\varepsilon+243\varepsilon^2$. Polynomially many bits
make this lower order uniformly. Internal-join selection has a
similar fixed Lipschitz bound. Correctness of the algebraic sum is
independent of which tree is selected.

\section{Complete conditional ECDLP transfer}
Combining the first two propositions gives
\[
 \log_2 C_m\le(19K+o(1))\frac{mb}{\sqrt{\log_2b}}.
\]
The factor 19 is a normalized information coefficient, not a
physical Boolean wire count. The complete inherited reduction gives
\[
 \log_2\mathbb ET\le
 \max\left\{\frac bm+19K\frac{mb}{\sqrt{\log_2b}},\frac{2b}{m}\right\}
 +o\left(\frac b{(\log_2b)^{1/4}}\right).
\]
Choose $m\sim(19K)^{-1/2}(\log_2b)^{1/4}$, rounded to an
admissible integer. The complete time and space coefficients are
$2\sqrt{19K}$ and $\sqrt{19K}$; workspace fits because $J\le K$.
Counting and whole-symbol witness pinning use $O(SQ)$ calls, still
polynomial, and preserve the root alphabet and graph bounds.

\section{Executed evidence and remaining work}
\texttt{verify\_c38\_simplex.py} tests 60 actual C38 instances,
36,608 recoded Fourier words, every local predicate on the reconstructed
witnesses, exact endpoint ledgers, independent tuple recovery, and
285 rejected mutations. Small exhaustive word tests establish the
implemented codec's bijection on the tested domains.
\texttt{verify\_adaptive\_simplex.py} compares 48 finite mode sums
against Cartesian enumeration, includes replicated coordinate families
and a nontrivial Strassen product over a nilpotent ring, and runs
70 complete active simplex contractions with every call checked.
\texttt{certify\_bounds.py} checks 30 matrix-parameter candidates,
the whole-domain support certificates, and 60 finite schedule cases,
including values adjacent to powers of two.

The adaptive code uses the explicit Strassen backend. It establishes
finite algebraic compatibility, not execution of the large published
matrix formulas. Its finite cache and table choices are diagnostic;
the theorem uses the stated sequential implementation when necessary
for the asymptotic space bound. A full unpinned C38 relation network
has not been contracted by this engine. The original foundational
proof chain still needs independent scrutiny.

The next substantial objective is a complete arithmetic-network
contractor or a structural change to the normalized mode/precision
cost. Improving selectors within the present coordinate-degree model
alone cannot reduce the limiting factor below $1/2$ and cannot change
the quarter-power balance. A stronger-power claim must replace a
specific premise of that model and pay for all newly introduced work.
