NEXT HYPOTHESIS -- NONUNIFORM DYADIC PACKET COORDINATES

Purpose: seek a real change in the normalized mode/precision spectrum,
not another optimization of the existing constant.

Candidate: replace consecutive packet coordinates by geometrically
spaced coordinates X={0,1,2,4,...,2^(n-2)}. At an odd local node, the
normalized Cauchy diagonal may gain quadratic rather than linear mode
valuations. This apparent gain is not yet an algorithmic improvement.

First falsification gate:
1. Form the DISTINCT signed-difference grid D=X-X and C(T)=product(T-d).
2. Compute v_2(C'(tau))-v_2(C'(0)) for every retained tau in D, starting
   with tau=1. This is a nonzero W-entry's constant-term valuation.
3. Negative values cannot be repaired just by shrinking the local jet
   variable T=tau+2^ell Z; their constant terms remain nonintegral.
4. Derive and charge any necessary common scaling, readout loss, degree
   increase, Hermite multiplicity, and number of coefficient entries.
5. Compare normalized diagonal gains against those losses. Reject any
   entropy claim that omits the local constant term or uses a multiset
   of differences as if it were a distinct interpolation grid.

Working algebra to verify, not yet a certified result:
For n>=3, with m=n-2, counting distinct differences suggests
    v_2(C'(0)) = (m^3+5*m)/3,
    v_2(C'(1)) = (n^2-n-4)/2.
If correct, the geometric candidate incurs a cubic negative constant
valuation, despite a quadratic diagonal gain. Test independently by
direct products, then prove the count or produce a counterexample.

Acceptance requires a complete improved resource recurrence, including
packing, coefficient extraction, precision and child construction.
A local spectral gain alone does not pass. If the geometric family
fails, distinguish that family from a lower bound against all nonuniform
coordinate sets and consider other grids or a different representation.
