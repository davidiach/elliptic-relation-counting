NEXT EXPERIMENT: THE ACTUAL DEFORMATION SPACE

The degree-only readout has verified finite savings, but cannot improve
the consecutive-grid geometric precision scale asymptotically. Avoid
another unpriced numerical optimization of that same objective.

First determine what polynomials the contractor actually produces.

1. Start with a complete small packet and literal binary selector
   polynomials. Generate each deformation polynomial obtained by fixing
   the source and target Boolean assignments. Evaluate W polynomials
   and the selector composition exactly over the rationals.

2. Compare with the native implementation on original assignments and
   several exact deformation values. Keep source weighting and padding
   in the definition. If a C38 restriction is used, label its scope.

3. Compute the exact rational span and a coefficient-lattice description
   after explicitly charging known common denominators. Do not assume a
   common polynomial factor from an individual W entry: the full
   selector composition includes constants and sums.

4. Optimize readout only on the proved space. The target functional is
   evaluation at zero on that space, not automatically the coordinate
   vector e_0 of the old monomial basis. Certify both reconstruction and
   optimality, with an explicit witness lying in the same space.

5. Include a legal tensor assignment designed to falsify every proposed
   universal relation. Compare general packet tensors, equality-copy
   restrictions, and actual C38 predicates rather than silently moving
   between those input classes.

Acceptance requires a uniform structural explanation and full access,
normalization, coefficient-dimension and recursion costs. A low-rank
finite sample or a gain from inputs smaller than a full packet is only
a diagnostic. A generic full span is a useful rejection result, not a
lower bound against all ECDLP algorithms.

Keep the general square-root-in-b target, and the missing complete C38
contraction/witness-recovery milestone, explicit throughout.
