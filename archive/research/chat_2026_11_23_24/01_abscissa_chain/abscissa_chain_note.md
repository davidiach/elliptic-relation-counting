# General-prime coefficient candidate: abscissa-only principal-ideal compiler

**Status:** Conditional research derivation, 23 November 2026. Not an end-to-end contraction benchmark or independent verification of finite-packet Assumption A.1.

## Claim

Use the curve/order class and inherited finite-packet, graph contraction, verified witness and one-pilot reduction assumptions of the v3 paper. For sufficiently large field bitlength b, an abscissa-only quartic compiler has leading word-edge budget (8+o(1))mb at arity m = Theta((log_2 b)^(1/4)). If the supplied contractor applies to this graph and the inherited reduction accepts the sampler and witness interface described here, the expected log-time coefficient is 2 sqrt(4K) = 12.39481702771492 and the log-space coefficient is sqrt(4K) = 6.19740851385746, where K = 2 pi sqrt(14 log_2 90)/(9 ln 2). No improvement in the asymptotic order b/(log_2 b)^(1/4) is asserted.

## 1. General-prime polynomial representation

Let L=log_2 b. Choose n=q1 q2=Theta(b/L^2) for distinct odd primes q1,q2=Theta(sqrt(b)/L), both dividing p-1 or neither dividing p-1; three candidate primes suffice. In the latter case set lambda=2 and gamma=2^(n^(-1) mod (p-1)) mod p. In the former set lambda=1 and choose gamma of exact order n in F_p. Put R=Z[X]/(X^n-lambda) and phi(A)=A(gamma) mod p. The irreducible polynomial relevant to gamma has degree d=n or d=(q1-1)(q2-1), respectively (Eisenstein or cyclotomic).

The kernel lattice in degree less than d has determinant p. Minkowski gives a nonzero f with coefficient bound sqrt(d) p^(1/d). Exact SVP can find it in 2^O(d log d) time. Shifts of f are independent in the irreducible rational quotient. In the cyclotomic case append the n-d shifts of Phi_n to obtain n independent short vectors in ker phi, using that Phi_(q1 q2) has coefficients in {-1,0,1}. Let S bound their coefficients, with log_2 S=b/d+O(log b).

Round the coordinates of the constant polynomial 8 n^2 S in this basis to integers to obtain M in ker phi. Its constant coefficient M0 is Theta(n^2 S), and M0-2 sum_{i>0}|Mi|=Theta(M0). The multiplication matrix A_M in R is strictly row diagonally dominant and ||A_M^(-1)||_infinity=O(1/M0). Its determinant D in absolute value is divisible by p, since R/(M) surjects onto F_p. Hadamard gives log_2 D<=n b/d+O(n log n)=b+O(b/L), so p^2>D for sufficiently large b. Thus D=pc with gcd(c,p)=1.

Let e=c*(c^(-1) mod p). Since D annihilates the additive group of R/(M), e is idempotent there and pe=0. The map x -> ex is a ring embedding of F_p into the e-component. A designated polynomial representative of ex is obtained by rounding the coordinates of ex in the basis A_M. Every coefficient has magnitude O(M0). Fix a public dyadic signed encoding containing all these designated representatives, of total length A=b+O(b/L) (A>=log_2 p by injectivity). It is not necessary to enforce global canonicity or component membership of arbitrary encoded representatives: valid designated lifts suffice for completeness, and evaluation phi gives soundness of every polynomial-identity witness.

## 2. Sampler and coverage

Apply an independent odd affine hash on the A-bit representation word of slot 1. For the other slots, choose ell free bits in Fourier-row owner order and fix every other bit independently uniformly. The factor base is obtained by enumerating all w=2^ell retained words, discarding any out-of-range coefficient representations if necessary, evaluating at gamma, lifting abscissas to rational points and deduplicating with slot labels retained. The graph admits all allowed representatives, not only the designated lifts.

For analysis, restrict to the unique designated lift of each field element. Its one-point acceptance probability is rho=w/2^A. In slot 1, two distinct designated words have joint acceptance at most rho^2. For fixed-bit slots the difference set of the retained words, mapped linearly into F_p, has size at most 2^s w, with s<=n+O(1) when runs are split at coefficient boundaries. The function x(P+D)-x(P) for nonidentity D has degree four, so the sum of joint acceptance probabilities over P is at most 4 rho 2^s w. This is the same mixed-slot second-moment argument as in derivations/strided_slots/results.md, replacing the binary modulus 2^b by 2^A.

For N=|E(F_p)| and lambda_0=N^(m-1)rho^m, choose the least dyadic w with N rho >= max(2 N^(1/m),20m). Then ell=b/m+O(b/L), lambda_0>=2^m, and m 2^s/w=o(1). Guarded tuples of any target outside E[2] whose internal prefix sums avoid E[2] have first moment at least (1-O(m/N))lambda_0; the excluded prefix and identity conditions discard O(m N^(m-2)) ordered full-group tuples. The second moment is bounded by rho^2(2w)^(2m-2)(1+2(m-1)2^s/w)+2lambda_0. Cauchy-Schwarz gives inverse targetwise success probability at most 2^O(m(1+A-b))=2^O(b/L^(3/4)) in the tuned regime. This is lower order in the final log-time exponent. Targets in E[2] must be skipped/rerandomized as appropriate to the inherited reduction; nonzero odd-prime-subgroup targets meet the condition.

## 3. Abscissa-only chain and rationality

Set X1=x1, Xm=x(T). For j=2,...,m impose S3(X_(j-1),xj,Xj)=0, where

S3(u,v,w)=(u-v)^2 w^2-2((u+v)(uv+a)+2c)w+(uv-a)^2-4c(u+v).

Over the algebraic closure, S3=0 is equivalent to existence of points with these finite abscissas and U+V=W. For distinct u,v this is the usual addition identity, obtained from [f(u)+f(v)-(w+u+v)(u-v)^2]^2=4f(u)f(v). For u=v and f(u) != 0 the polynomial is linear in w and its root is x(2U); if f(u)=0 its value is f'(u)^2 !=0. This also handles tangency. All finite x in F_p have lifts with Frobenius + or - identity on the point. If U+V is rational and is not two-torsion, both U,V must be rational: two non-rational summands would yield an anti-rational sum, and one non-rational summand and one rational summand cannot have rational sum. Starting from the prescribed target and working backward therefore recovers rational signed leaves provided no internal partial sum lies in E[2]. The signs are recoverable by testing at most four pairs per step. Sign-paired masks are crucial. This decides existence, not exact multiplicity of ordered signed tuples.

## 4. Exact removal of two-torsion states

There are at most three finite abscissas of E[2]. In an odd-order rational group there are none. Otherwise use inclusion-exclusion over the m-2 internal states. To pin a field value x, enumerate every allowed polynomial A satisfying phi(A)=x; do not merely pin its designated lift.

Let Lambda=ker phi in Z^n, with explicit basis B given by p e0 and e_i-(gamma^i mod p)e0 for i>0. The principal ideal lattice A_M Z^n has index D/p in Lambda. Hermite normal form of B^(-1)A_M enumerates the D/p cosets. For a fixed x, start each coset at x e0 plus its representative, then reduce it by rounding in A_M. Its resulting vector r has norm O(M0). Every bounded representative in this coset is r+A_M z with ||z||_infinity=O(1), since ||A_M^(-1)||_infinity=O(1/M0). Enumerating the constant-size range per coordinate is exhaustive and disjoint after range checking. Thus the full list of reps of x costs 2^O(b/L) time and space. For each set of pinned internal states, enumerate all their bad values and all bounded reps and sum disjoint exact pin counts. The total cost multiplier including inclusion-exclusion is 2^O(mb/L)=2^o(b/L^(1/4)). This implements a nonnegative guarded count and applies under arbitrary other edge pins. Exact integer count precision O(number of Boolean edges) suffices. Bitwise witness extraction remains polynomially many such count calls.

## 5. Quartic graph ledger

Use bounded polynomial representatives of all abscissas and public a,c. If a field residual vanishes, designated lifts give S3(U,V,W)=M Q in R for some integral Q. Conversely this equality for any allowed lifts implies the field equation upon applying phi. Quartic residual coefficient magnitudes are O(n^3 M0^4) with public constant factors (lambda<=2); the exact quotient satisfies ||Q||_infinity=O(n^3 M0^3) by diagonal dominance. Choose an auxiliary prime q congruent to -1 modulo 8n (a polynomial bound follows from the inherited effective prime-progression result), and q^h greater than twice a public bound on the coefficients of S3-MQ. Thus W=log_2(q^h)=4b/d+O(log b). Take a primitive n-th root omega in the quadratic unramified extension of Z/q^h, with conjugation omega->omega^(-1), and real beta with beta^n=lambda; the latter exists because gcd(n,q^(h-1)(q-1))=1. The transform of beta^i A_i converts folded polynomial multiplication into pointwise multiplication. Distinct root differences are units. Enforce S3=M Q at all frequencies and use the coefficient bound for exact integral equality.

Factor the transform n=q1 q2. Row owners check actual coefficient ranges, pins and masks after inverse first-stage transform; the middle processors complete the second transform and check all identities. Conjugate compression sends exactly q1 base words per row (one real self-paired frequency, one two-component extension word per pair). There are no arithmetic output arrays. Each full array uses nW=(4+o(1))b bits. Internal abscissas give m-2 full arrays, quotients m-1, and leaf arrays other than the affine pilot have active row fraction O(1/m+1/q2). Pilot, range, and linking costs are lower order; the original short inverse-hash compiler or a full hash on the A-bit word can be used for the single pilot. The graph has degree O(m sqrt(n) W)=sqrt(b) polylog(b), polylogarithmic parallel multiplicity and polynomial-time entry access after the charged preprocessing. The exact applicability of the supplied finite-packet specification, including its alphabet and precision conventions, still deserves an independent integration audit.

## 6. Outer balance and limitations

The proposed leading graph budget is (8+o(1))mb. The inherited conversion would give log_2 C_m <=(4K+o(1))mb/sqrt(L). The B=2mw bound on distinct factor-base labels satisfies log_2 B=b/m+O(b/L+log m). Preprocessing 2^O(b/L), retries and torsion pin enumeration 2^O(mb/L), and witness reconstruction are lower order. Applying the inherited one-pilot reduction requires the same deterministic selection rule in training and fresh queries; choose a fixed edge pinning and sign-reconstruction order, deduplicate the factor base, retain projected slot/point labels and verify witnesses. Subject to that transfer, the outer log-time terms are b/m+4K mb/sqrt(L) and 2b/m. Balancing gives m~L^(1/4)/sqrt(4K) and the coefficients in the claim.

This is not a proof of the finite-packet assumption, a proof of an asymptotically smaller logarithmic power, an exact signed-count decoder, a novelty certification against the entire literature, or a practical runtime estimate. The previous project geometric supplement already describes the quartic Semaev equation; the candidate contribution is integrating it with principal-ideal arithmetic, sign-symmetric redundant masks and the charged exact torsion correction. The full numeric contraction/reduction integration is still a required review item.
