NEXT EXPERIMENT: DEGREE-AWARE LINEAR JET READOUT

Motivation
The full Hermite functional is unique on degree < total_jet_count, but
may be nonoptimal on the smaller actual deformation degree. The new
enumeration bounds explicitly do not cover optimized functionals that
use these linear dependencies.

Finite model
For degree d, make the integer jet evaluation matrix
  A[(tau,j),k] = binom(k,j) * tau^(k-j) * 2^(ell_tau*j)
for 0<=k<=d, 0<=j<q_tau, with zero entries when j>k.
Find exact rational weights w with w*A=e_0, minimizing the common
2-adic denominator exponent L (all 2^L*w integral at 2).
This optimizes a LINEAR readout on arbitrary rational polynomials of
degree <=d. It is not automatically a lower bound for nonlinear readout
with additional coefficient-integrality or tensor-support promises.

Proposed certification
1. Choose any confluent Vandermonde square minor of size d+1 by taking
   initial jet prefixes totaling d+1. Its determinant valuation C is
   known from node differences and local scales.
2. Work modulo 2^K with K>C+2. Use minimal-valuation pivots and unit row
   and column operations to diagonalize the row module. Transform e_0
   by the same column operations.
3. The minimal denominator exponent is
   max_i(valuation(diagonal_i)-valuation(transformed_target_i),0).
   The explicit full-rank minor bounds all relevant invariant factors;
   values beyond the finite working precision must not be guessed.
4. Recover modular row coefficients, then lift to an EXACT rational
   identity. Correct the residual using the known square minor. Since
   the residual is divisible by 2^K, its correction has enough valuation
   to preserve the proposed denominator bound.
5. Retain both the exact rational identity and the modular obstruction
   at L-1. Compare against the existing full Hermite coefficients.

First cases
Small consecutive grids and the balanced geometric-cluster grids from
iteration 007, including overdetermined jet sets. Include controls where
the full degree equals total_jet_count-1, where the readout is unique.

Do not confuse this linear-operator objective with computing only the
constant coefficient modulo 2 on integer-coefficient polynomials; that
is a different module/precision problem. Actual deformation coefficients
have known dyadic denominators, and their structure must be charged if
used in a later, stronger model.

Acceptance
Exact reconstruction and optimality certificates first. An asymptotic
gain requires a constructive uniform readout, its construction cost,
precision profile, all coefficient dimensions, and the full recursive
and ECDLP balance. Keep the square-root target unchanged.
