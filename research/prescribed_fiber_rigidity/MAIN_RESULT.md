# Target certificates from prescribed sum fibers

**Two representations can overcome the pointwise-flat single-pair obstruction.** Suppose the explicit genuine system \(B\lambda=b_B\) has modular corank one and the short-tuple sumset has \(3s>2r+1\). Let \(v\) be an integer labeling, zero at each block reference, and \(h\) a scalar with \(Bv=h b_B\bmod r\). Define \(R(v)\) as the sum of the integer ranges of the labels within the blocks. If \(0<2R(v)<r\), two triples over **any prescribed target**, relative to **any fixed identity triple**, suffice for a deterministically constructed certificate in the given row space. Compute a positive integer sum of squared score differences; only ordinary closed counts with at most two pinned occurrences are needed per summand. With the original Stage I predicates the charged worst-case bit time and space are \(2^{O(b/(\log b)^{1/4})}\), conditional on LZR 2.2 and on obtaining the explicit input subsystem and lift in budget. For a square rational basis, testing its primitive augmented lift costs polynomial time in the explicit data. No assumption about modular Gram noncancellation or scalar modal concentration enters this proof.

Concurrent PR #10 supplies an instructive test. Its supplied bad basis has an **exact integer solution** with entries between \(-3\) and \(1\). Rational elimination recovers it from the rows alone. The theorem then covers all targets in the two-pin count model, including targets invisible to every individual doubled-tuple pair. On the same \(C_{101}\) fixture, 49 of 101 displacements have an individual certificate; two target triples certified **101 of 101**, against one fixed identity triple. This gives a rigorous positive result on the structure underlying that obstruction. Its randomly augmented asymptotic blocks have no exhibited cheap membership circuit, so the count reduction alone does not give them a quarter-power bit bound. **Constructive result.** Start with the original Stage I blocks and a genuinely known explicit system with row space \(W\) over the prime-order scalar field. In each indexed block, put two labels in the same class when the difference of their unit incidence vectors belongs to \(W\). Let \(\eta\) be the product, across blocks, of the fraction contained in a largest class. Gaussian elimination computes this number from the given rows. For each fixed rational \(\eta_0>0\), if \(\eta\ge\eta_0\), every target on sufficiently large eligible inputs has a uniformly and deterministically constructible certificate in \(W\) in
\[
 2^{O_{\eta_0}(b/(\log b)^{1/4})}
\]
bit time and space, including Stage I and polynomial work on the explicit subsystem, assuming its construction and size fit that budget. The sole algorithmic premise is LZR Theorem 2.2. The input-side concentration hypothesis is additional. This procedure extracts **zero additional relation rows**. It can work at positive corank.

The construction works more generally for arbitrary explicitly listed retained labels of constant joint fraction on the original low-energy blocks. A Fourier estimate gives positive density of retained tuples in each target fiber after constantly many copies. An odd Bonferroni truncation gives a signed integer count that can be evaluated with constantly many pins per query. Positive-prefix search finds an actual retained representation. Apply this at zero and at the target. All chosen tuples have the same quotient incidence, so subtraction belongs to \(W\). For summed lost fractions below \(1/4\), three copies and the first-order weight \(1-H\) suffice. [Proofs and cost](manuscript.pdf)

**Completely unresolved blocks may be omitted.** More generally, it suffices that one nonempty subset of blocks has untrimmed chi-square at most \(1/7\) and joint retained fraction at least \(\eta_0\), with all quotient classes computed in the original \(W\). At most \(2^m\) cheap collision guards find such a subset. On the existing deletion-resilient seed this reduces to sorting the class fractions and taking the largest \(m-\lfloor\alpha m\rfloor\), for fixed \(\alpha<1/2\). This strictly enlarges the checkable condition: there are genuine subsystems on these actual masks where the retained product is one and the all-block product tends to zero. Efficient construction of those example subsystems is a separate hypothesis. [Omitted-block corollary](manuscript.pdf)

**Unconditional structural result.** Suppose the possible short sums occupy \(s\) elements of a cyclic group of prime order \(r\). If \(3s>2r+1\), the integer differences of triple incidences on **any one prescribed target**, even restricted to pairs sharing their identical third tuple, generate exactly the lattice \(\ker\pi\), where \(\pi(e_{i,a})=a-b_i\) for a reference \(b_i\) in each block. A nonaffine score has at least \(2s-r-1\) mixed two-sums. Intersecting these with the complements at the prescribed target yields at least \(3s-2r-1\) distinct choices for the shared third value. Interval examples over integer scores attain the stated thresholds. The cokernel is cyclic of order \(r\). [Integer lattice analysis](INTEGER_LATTICE_ANALYSIS.md)

With one genuine nonzero shift in \(W\), target triples relative to a single fixed identity triple affinely span \(\mathbb F_r^D/W\). Thus at most \(d+1\) target triples suffice for an affine certificate at corank \(d\). This existence bound alone has no affordable worst-case construction. PR #9 already obtains \(d+1\) using all target-displacement pairs on a surjective domain. The new claim restricts to one specified value fiber with a common fixed identity reference. [Target identifiability](TARGET_IDENTIFIABILITY.md) · [PR #9](https://github.com/davidiach/elliptic-relation-counting/pull/9)

**Overall assessment.** The constructive theorem is a restricted target-identifiability result. The general prime-order input class still lacks a deterministic algorithm with any proved fixed positive logarithmic saving here. No available bound on residual dimension ensures the needed concentration. The algebraic results apply to the actual Stage I density, and the sufficient concentration can be checked on each explicit output. The constants for the general retained-set theorem can be extremely large. Priority for the precise combined statements is provisional.
