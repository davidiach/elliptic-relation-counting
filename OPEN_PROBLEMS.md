# Remaining mathematical questions

Only the guarded short-list branch still needs a rank proof or an eligible counterexample. The exhaustive branch always succeeds; the unguarded order-two failure is repaired by a single further grid point. In the short-list regime each elementary pinned displacement convolution survives at every torus point. A proof must control its coefficient at $P$ and the signed sums of those coefficients. An average over displacements can be evaluated and searched deterministically, but the selected displacements carry unknown logarithms. See [Exact neighboring fibers](NEIGHBORING_FIBERS.md).

At the prescribed $P$, Propositions 3 and 4 of that note recover full shift rank after summing both neighboring fibers with the group value and two slot pins retained. In particular a residual direction cannot disappear solely by summing the unweighted successors for each source. The open implication is from vanishing at the coupled evaluations to a contradiction, without assuming the separate group values or source coefficients can be read off.

The deterministic list in [Deterministic points](DETERMINISTIC_POINTS.md) reduces its own full-rank question to residual directions in $\ker E$. It suffices that the ideal generated by the coordinates of $Bu$ contain a nonzero polynomial of at most $C$ terms and individual degree at most $2^{\lfloor b/3\rfloor}$ for each such nonzero $u$. A proof may instead establish rank directly on the proposed points. Dense half-sum coverage forces mixed fibers for every residual direction. Any argument allowing arbitrary omission of a small fraction of valid rows faces the cancellation theorem in that note.

**First priority.** Given the deterministically constructed LZR blocks and two input points, construct a hitting list for the determinant in MAIN_RESULT.md. Budget both construction and evaluation at $2^{O(b/(\log b)^{1/4})}$ bit time. It suffices to accumulate enough row information from several matrices that their vertical stack has rank $N$, since all right sides are consistent. No individual matrix need be invertible for this relaxation. Guaranteed rank of the stack, rather than average rank, is the missing statement.

* **Single-candidate target.** A hitting set for the scalar one-pin polynomial under all possible surviving $y$ would also suffice. A nonzero value allows deterministic extraction without new random bits. The permitted coefficients lie in the column span of a sparse incidence matrix supported on a fixed elliptic shift. Exploit that promise; a generic linear-algebra black-box hitting set for *all* coefficient arrays must have at least as many evaluations as the dimension tested.
* **Candidate direction compression.** Add the $(w+1)$-multiplication rows first and restrict a candidate to their affine kernel. The relevant dimension is at most $N\log(w+1)/\log r$. Investigate deterministic rank concentration for the projected incidence rows. A generic dimension reduction of this size saves only a polynomial factor in $b$.
* **Precision and ranks in the residual route.** The one-step expansion nearly attains the $1/4$ saving at the original arity. Reaching an exact $1/4$ here requires removing the growing-group label-array cost or avoiding full root mode enumeration. A subsequent generic residual contraction still has $\rho=\Theta(\log m)$. Either specialize its input tensors or bypass it. The Gram construction gives one bypass at a supplied point.
* **Weighted norm evaluation.** Construct a charged trace on the joint relation algebra that treats repeated points, parity-specific masks, signed labels and canonical dyadic lifts together. A small number of formal norm operations on an exponentially large algebra is insufficient; specify dimension, entry access, setup and bit costs.
* **Source assurance.** Obtain the accepted Lean export and matching environment, rebuild it, trace the main theorem's imports, and inspect a transitive axiom report. Compare each field of the assumed Appendix D interface to a proved machine-level upstream theorem. Public statement digests make future comparison possible.

A uniform positive intermediate saving $\delta<1/4$ follows if an instance-specific list is constructible and testable in $2^{O(b/(\log b)^\delta)}$ time. A coefficient improvement in an expected pilot calculation or a lower cost for partial expansion alone does not imply this result.
